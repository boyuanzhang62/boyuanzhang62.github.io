THREAD Lab

THREAD stands for Techniques for High-performance, Resource-Efficient Analytics and Data.

Our vision is to extend high-performance optimization to a broader range of applications. We develop GPU-accelerated and data-efficient computing techniques for scientific computing, machine learning systems, quantum computing, and other emerging data-intensive workloads.
