About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Kentucky and director of the THREAD Lab.

My research focuses on High-Performance Computing, GPU-accelerated computing, data reduction, and performance optimization for large-scale scientific applications.

I received my Ph.D. from Indiana University Bloomington, my M.S. from the University of Southern California, and my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
